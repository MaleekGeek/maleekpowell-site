When you're stressed and behind, the walk is the first thing to go. You tell yourself you'll move when you have a proper hour.

That hour almost never comes. And the research says you don't need it.

## Ten minutes, and your memory sharpens

In 2018, researchers in Japan and California had 36 young adults pedal an exercise bike for ten minutes. Very light effort, nowhere near a workout. On another day, the same people just sat for ten minutes.

Straight after the easy pedalling, they did better on a memory test that asks you to tell apart things that look almost the same. Brain scans showed the memory part of the brain talking more with other areas linked to detailed memory.

Ten minutes. Easy pace. And the change showed up right away.

It was a small study, with young, healthy people, and it looked at what happens right after moving, not over years. But it's a good reason to stop believing that movement only counts if you suffer through it.

## A little movement, a lower risk of depression

A bigger review in 2022 pulled together 15 studies that followed about 190,000 adults over time.

People who did the recommended amount of activity, about two and a half hours of brisk walking a week, had a 25% lower risk of depression than people who did none.

And people who did *half* that, about an hour and fifteen minutes a week, still had an 18% lower risk. That works out to around eleven minutes a day.

The biggest difference was between doing nothing and doing a little. Going way past the recommended amount didn't add much more.

One honest note. Studies like this show a link, not proof. It could partly be that people who feel low move less. And walking isn't a treatment for depression. If you're dealing with depression, keep working with your doctor or counsellor. Movement can sit alongside that. It doesn't replace it.

## How to fit it in

You don't need new shoes or an app. You need ten minutes and a door.

- Walk after lunch, before you go back to the screen.
- Take the phone call standing up and walking.
- Park further away. Get off a stop early.
- When the kids are at practice, walk the field instead of sitting in the car.

And don't make it a punishment. If all you've got today is five minutes around the block, take the five.
